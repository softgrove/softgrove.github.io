GoutLog

Find your gout triggers

One bad meal. A missed water glass. A stressful week. Gout flares rarely come from nowhere, but in the moment it's hard to know which factor did it.

Download on theApp Store

What you can track

  • Flares: joint affected, severity, and symptoms (swelling, redness, heat)
  • Uric acid readings: mg/dL, with source (lab or home meter)
  • Meals: purine level of foods, alcohol type (none / beer / wine / spirits)
  • Contributing factors: hydration, stress, sleep quality
  • Free notes for anything that doesn't fit a field

Questions

Is GoutLog free?

The core app is free with no account needed — Log every flare, meal, and uric acid reading. Browse your full history. See your trend charts — including uric acid levels over time — across weeks and months. Nothing locked behind a paywall just to use the core app. Premium ($6.99/month or $39.99/year after a 7-day trial) adds the analysis features.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. GoutLog never uploads, syncs, shares, or sells your data — there is no account and no cloud backend.

Does GoutLog require an account?

No. You can start logging the moment the app opens — there is no sign-up, no email, and nothing to create.

Does GoutLog give medical advice?

No. GoutLog is a personal tracking tool only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions.
