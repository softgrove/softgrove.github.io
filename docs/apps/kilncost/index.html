KilnCost

Kiln firing cost calculator

See what a firing costs before you press start. Pick your kiln and cone, and KilnCost shows the estimated price and duration of the firing — $10.21 and 8h 31m, not a guess after the bill arrives.

Download on theApp Store

What you can track

  • 24 Skutt KilnMaster Touchscreen and L&L Easy-Fire models, in 49 voltage configurations
  • Power figures transcribed from the manufacturers' published spec tables
  • Or enter a custom kiln by kilowatts or volts × amps, with 2.5″ or 3″ brick
  • Orton cone chart built in, cones 022 through 10 (self-supporting regular cones)
  • Final ramp at 15, 60 or 150°C per hour — cone temperatures follow the ramp speed, as Orton publishes them
  • Bisque or glaze, plus an optional hold

Questions

Is KilnCost free?

Cataloging stones and blades and logging sessions are free, with no account and no trial clock. A one-time $6.99 Lifetime unlock adds Blade Card sharing and CSV export — there is no subscription and no renewal.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. KilnCost never uploads what you record, and never shares or sells your data — there is no account and no cloud backend. Some apps send anonymous usage counts (no identifiers) so we can see which features get used; each app's privacy policy spells out exactly what, if anything, is sent.

Does KilnCost require an account?

No. You can start logging the moment the app opens — there is no sign-up, no email, and nothing to create.
