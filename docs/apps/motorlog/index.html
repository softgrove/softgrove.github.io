MotorLog

Mileage, service & gas log

You know you changed the oil a few months back, but not exactly when, and definitely not how many miles ago.

Download on theApp Store

What you can track

  • Fuel fill-ups: date, gallons, price per gallon, odometer — MPG calculated automatically
  • Service records: oil changes, tire rotations, brake jobs, anything you want to remember
  • Repair history: parts replaced, shop name, cost, notes
  • Mileage logs: great for tax deductions if you drive for work
  • Multiple vehicles: track your car, truck, or motorcycle separately

Private by design

Everything you record stays on your device. MotorLog never uploads what you record, and never shares or sells your data. No account needed — delete the app and your entries are gone. Questions

Is MotorLog free?

The core app is free, with no account needed. Log every fill-up and service. Browse your full vehicle history. See basic fuel economy stats. Nothing locked behind a paywall just to use the core app. Premium ($6.99/month or $39.99/year after a 7-day trial) adds the analysis features.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. MotorLog never uploads what you record, and never shares or sells your data — there is no account and no cloud backend. Some apps send anonymous usage counts (no identifiers) so we can see which features get used; each app's privacy policy spells out exactly what, if anything, is sent.

Does MotorLog require an account?

No. You can start logging the moment the app opens — there is no sign-up, no email, and nothing to create.
