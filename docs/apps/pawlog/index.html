PawLog

Never miss a vet or dose

Your dog had a vet visit in March. Was it March 3rd or March 18th? Did they update the rabies vaccine or just the heartworm? What's the current flea medication, and when does it need to be given again?

Download on theApp Store

What you can track

  • Vet visits: date, clinic, reason, notes, and vet's name
  • Vaccines and boosters: which vaccine, date given, next due date
  • Medications: name, dosage, frequency, start and end dates
  • Weight check-ins: watch your pet's weight over time
  • Health notes: anything else — symptoms, behavioral changes, diet changes
  • Multiple pets: dogs, cats, or both — tracked separately

Questions

Is PawLog free?

The core app is free with no account needed — Log every vet visit and medication. Browse your pet's full history. See basic stats. Nothing locked behind a paywall just to use the core app. Premium ($6.99/month or $39.99/year after a 7-day trial) adds the analysis features.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. PawLog never uploads, syncs, shares, or sells your data — there is no account and no cloud backend.

Does PawLog require an account?

No. You can start logging the moment the app opens — there is no sign-up, no email, and nothing to create.
