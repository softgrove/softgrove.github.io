PlantLog

Never forget to water

You watered the fiddle leaf fig recently. Or was that the pothos? And when did you last fertilize anything?

Download on theApp Store

What you can track

  • Watering: date, amount, and notes — see exactly when each plant was last watered
  • Fertilizing: product, dilution, and frequency
  • Repotting and pruning: dates and pot sizes
  • Health notes: new growth, yellowing leaves, pests — anything worth recording
  • Multiple plants: every plant in your home or garden, tracked separately

Pricing, plainly

Questions

Is PlantLog free?

The core app is free with no account needed — Add all your plants. Log every watering and care event. Browse each plant's full history. See watering trends and care frequency charts. Set a daily reminder to check in. Nothing locked behind a paywall just to use the core app. Premium ($6.99/month or $39.99/year after a 7-day trial) adds the analysis features.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. PlantLog never uploads, syncs, shares, or sells your data — there is no account and no cloud backend.

Does PlantLog require an account?

No. You can start logging the moment the app opens — there is no sign-up, no email, and nothing to create.
