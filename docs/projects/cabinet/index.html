Project details

These are the carcass parts for a standard frameless (European style) base cabinet. The cabinet box is 34.5" tall so that a 1.5" countertop brings the finished height to 36". Sides are 23.25" deep, leaving 3/4" at the back for a recessed 1/4" back panel and scribe.

The bottom and stretchers run between the sides, so they are 28.5" long. Two 4" stretchers at the top replace a full top panel, which saves material and gives you a place to screw down the counter.

Cutting sequence

  1. Decide on the toe kick

    A notched side needs the full 34.5" height. A cabinet on a separate base can drop the sides to 30.5".

  2. Rip the deep parts together

    Rip 23.25" for the sides and bottom in one setup so the cabinet front stays flush.

  3. Cut the stretchers from the offcut

    The 4" stretchers fit in the strip left after the deep rips.

  4. Label parts as you cut

    Mark the front edge of the sides and bottom so grain direction stays consistent.

Questions

Will the doors fit on the same sheet?

Two 14.875 × 34.25 doors add about 7 sq ft. Add them as a part above and calculate; with the toe kick included you will usually need a second sheet.

Why 23.25" deep instead of 24"?

A 24" finished depth with a 3/4" recess for the back panel and the door thickness in front is the usual layout. Change the side depth if your back panel sits differently.
